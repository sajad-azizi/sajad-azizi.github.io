Short pulses.
Surprisingly slow electrons.

Looking ahead

What can we learn beyond timing?

Electron yields and time delays leave part of the wave’s angular phase undetermined. My proposed research asks whether an infrared probe can recover that information, and how accurately the probe must be understood.
